FAQ

Questions businesses usually ask.

Do you create fake reviews? +
No. LocalCred focuses on helping businesses request genuine feedback from real customers. We don't manufacture reviews, buy reviews, or manipulate ratings.
Can you guarantee more 5-star reviews? +
No legitimate reputation service can guarantee what customers will say. Our job is to make it easier for satisfied customers to leave genuine feedback and to help your business respond professionally to all types of reviews.
Do you delete negative reviews? +
We don't attempt to remove legitimate criticism. If content appears to violate a platform's policies, we can evaluate it separately through our performance-based dispute service.
Do I have to manage the reviews myself? +
No. Depending on your package, LocalCred can monitor new reviews, prepare or manage responses, provide alerts, and deliver recurring reputation reports.
What businesses are a good fit? +
We are particularly focused on high-consideration local businesses such as home services, auto repair, moving companies, wedding services, veterinary clinics, and similar businesses where customers frequently compare providers online.
How do we get started? +
Start with a reputation audit. We'll review your current online reputation and identify the highest-impact opportunities before recommending a plan.
