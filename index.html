The problem

Your reputation is being evaluated before you ever speak to the customer.

A potential customer can compare your business with several nearby competitors before they ever call, click, or walk through your door.

01

Customers compare businesses side by side.

Rating, review count, recent feedback, and response activity all form part of the picture customers see.

02

A high rating doesn't tell the whole story.

Recurring complaints, outdated reviews, and unanswered feedback can reveal opportunities that a star rating alone won't show.

03

Reputation management can't be a one-time task.

New reviews appear continuously. The businesses that manage reputation well treat it as an ongoing process.
